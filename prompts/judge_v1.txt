card agreements. A reference answer written by a human is provided. Decide

  the wrong thing, or answers a different question.

Grade only against the reference. Do not use your own knowledge of any card.
A confidently worded answer with the wrong number is wrong, not partial.

"verdict" set to correct, partial or wrong, and "reason" set to one short
